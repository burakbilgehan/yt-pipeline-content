06 · Layout contracts

Where things go — always

Inconsistency across scenes was the #1 critic failure in v4. These six contracts fix the position of every recurring element. Every template must honour them or explicitly document an exception (e.g. OpeningScene breaks LC-1).

LC-1

Title anchor — varsayılan sol-alt

Başlık içeren sahneler (öneri, kural değil)

Başlıklar için varsayılan konum sol-alttır. Bu kesin bir kural değil, güçlü bir öneri — tutarlılık açısından değerli, ama sahnenin veri düzeni farklı bir konumu gerektiriyorsa sapılabilir.

  • Eyebrow (11px, pembe, uppercase) başlığın 4px üstünde
  • Başlık Montserrat — 52–64px, uzunluğa göre
  • Maksimum genişlik frame'in %66'sı
  • BigStatScene başlığı sol-üste taşıyabilir (LC-3 notu)

Güncellendi (Burak): Sol-alt artık "tartışmasız kural" değil, "sağlam default". Sapma gerekiyorsa belgelensin.

LC-2

Citation anchor — bottom-right

All data scenes (not narrative)

Citations always appear bottom-right, 2% from bottom, 3% from right. They use 22px Inter at var(--text-muted).

  • Prefix "Source:" in mono, same size, 8px right-margin
  • Source body in Inter regular (not bold, not italic)
  • If source has a URL, underline suppressed — it's print-style
  • Max 2 lines. If longer, abbreviate: "IMF WEO, Oct 2024"

LC-3

Hero number — center, never shares focus

BigStatScene · StatWallSequence

The hero number (160px mono) centers both horizontally and vertically in the primary area. Nothing else competes for focus at that vertical position.

  • Descriptor sits below the number with 32px gap
  • Never pair two hero numbers in the same frame — use split-scene instead
  • The number can break LC-1 title placement: eyebrow moves to top-left when hero is present

LC-4

Chart canvas — 3/4 frame, right-dominant

BarChartScene · LineTrendScene · HorseRaceChart

Chart scenes give ~75% of the frame to the chart canvas, with eyebrow + title stacked in the left 25% column. Gridlines and axis labels are inside the chart canvas — not floating.

  • Chart canvas: 72% wide × 86% tall, right-aligned with 2% gutter
  • Title column: 24% wide, left side, vertically centered on chart
  • Citation per LC-2
  • Direct data labels preferred over legends (VB-5)

LC-5

Duel split — 50/50 vertical, value-dominant

DuelSplitScene · BeforeAfterCards

Two-value comparisons split the frame down the middle. Each half contains: label (top), big number (center), descriptor (below). A 2% center gutter separates them — no dividing line.

  • Left half = pink data · Right half = blue data (VB-2 exception allowed if diverging)
  • Proportional bar (VB-3) spans full frame width at the base, showing ratio
  • If a delta between the two matters, put it as a chip in the center gutter

LC-6

Ranked list — left column, bars extend right

TopNListScene · RankingResortScene

Ranked lists always align with rank numbers left-flush, item labels immediately right of rank, and proportional bars extending right across ~60% of the frame.

  • Rank number: 11px mono, sage, 44px gutter
  • Item label: 22–28px Inter medium
  • Value: tabular mono, right-aligned at bar end
  • Stagger entrance 10f between items (VB-6)
  • No stripes, no alternating-row backgrounds — data-ink (VB-5)

Spacing scale — the 8-point grid

All spacing is a multiple of 8px. This is non-negotiable — it's what makes visually-related templates feel like siblings.
