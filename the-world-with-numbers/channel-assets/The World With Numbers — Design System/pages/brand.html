Positioning & voice

For people unsatisfied with surface-level knowledge. Every video delivers a clean, fact-first perspective on how things are ranked, measured, and compared — through the raw weight of data, not opinion.

Personality — five words

  • PreciseEvery number cited. Every chart mathematically truthful. No rounding that obscures truth.
  • CuriousGenuinely finds a GDP chart more interesting than most conversations. The drive is discovery, not argument.
  • UnderstatedNever shouts. No exclamation marks. No hype language. The data carries the weight.
  • AuthoritativeWell-researched, confident, calm. Earns trust through rigor — not through louder claims.
  • AtmosphericA dimly lit room. A single low tone. Montserrat at 140px. Silence does structural work.

Reference comparison

The Economist's writing voice × BBC documentary's visual language × a data scientist who also appreciates good typography. There is no hype. The numbers are the drama.

PillarPremiseExample topics

Index ReleasesTime-sensitive coverage of major global report drops. Search-discovery engine.Global Peace Index · Corruption Perceptions · HDI · Press Freedom
