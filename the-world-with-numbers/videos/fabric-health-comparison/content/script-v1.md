Every time you wash a polyester fleece jacket, it releases roughly one hundred and ten thousand microplastic fibers into the water. <break time="1200ms"/>

They've been found in human lung tissue. [pause short] In human brains. [pause short] In placentas. [pause long]

The safest fabrics based on the research — organic cotton, linen, hemp, Tencel, and superfine merino wool. [pause]

Wool absorbs up to thirty percent of its weight in moisture without feeling damp. [pause short] Polyester does the opposite — it creates a sealed layer that traps sweat against your skin and accelerates chemical absorption. [pause]
