📊 Key findings:
• 69% of global textile production is synthetic — projected to reach 75% by 2030
• Researchers found 93 out of 98 targeted chemicals in clothing samples
• Children's clothing had 3x higher chemical concentrations than adult garments
• "Eco-friendly" garments averaged MORE formaldehyde than conventional ones (10.4 vs 8.23 mg/kg)
• Sweat-soaked polyester activewear delivers BPA at 125–570x the EU safe limit
• Polyester fleece sheds 7,360 fibers/m²/L per wash vs 87 for standard knit — 85x more
• Synthetic microfibers persist 58–1,200 years in the environment
• 100% cotton with stain-resistant finish: 19,100 ng/g PFAS vs untreated blend: 1.7 ng/g
