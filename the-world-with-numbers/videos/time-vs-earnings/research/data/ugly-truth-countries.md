## Track 2: Non-OECD "Ugly Truth" Countries

### Data Sources Used
1. **Hours worked:** Our World in Data 2023 (Penn World Table + ILO) — "per worker" column
2. **Income proxy:** Since there's no OECD PPP wage equivalent for these countries, we use a combination of:
   - ILO Global Wage Report data
   - World Bank GNI per capita PPP
   - Wikipedia "Asian/African countries by average wage" — Net PPP monthly figures × 12
   - IMF GDP per capita PPP as upper-bound context
3. **Critical caveat:** These are NOT "average annual wages for full-time equivalent employees" like the OECD figures. They mix formal/informal sector, include vastly different labor compositions.

### India (IND)
- **Hours:** 2,383/yr (Our World in Data 2023, from ILO/PWT)
- **Wage data:** 
  - Average monthly wage: ₹17,166 gross for urban self-employed males (2022) [Wikipedia Asian wages]
  - Net PPP monthly: $718 (urban males) [Wikipedia Asian wages, IMF PPP conversion]
  - Annual PPP: ~$8,616 (urban males), rural males much lower at ~$4,848
  - ILO mean nominal monthly earnings: ~$186/mo (2022) = ~$2,232/yr nominal
  - GNI per capita PPP (World Bank 2023): ~$9,820
  - GDP per capita PPP (IMF 2026): ~$14,000
- **Best estimate for chart: ~$8,600 PPP (formal sector avg)**
- **$/hr:** $8,600 ÷ 2,383 = **$3.61**
- **Notes:** Massive informal sector (~80% of workforce). Formal sector wages much higher than informal. The $8,600 figure represents formal sector workers, which is already optimistic. Real average including informal is likely $4,000-6,000.

### Bangladesh (BGD)
- **Hours:** 2,383/yr (Our World in Data 2023)
- **Wage data:**
  - Average monthly wage: Tk 26,867 = ~$243/mo gross (2023) [Wikipedia Asian wages]
  - Net PPP monthly: $813 [Wikipedia Asian wages]
  - Annual PPP: ~$9,756 (PPP adjusted) — but this seems high
  - ILO: ~$137/mo mean nominal earnings
  - GNI per capita PPP (World Bank 2023): ~$7,070
  - GDP per capita PPP (IMF 2026): ~$9,500
- **Best estimate for chart: ~$7,000 PPP (using GNI per capita as proxy)**
- **$/hr:** $7,000 ÷ 2,383 = **$2.94**
- **Notes:** Garment industry dominates exports. Very large informal sector. Wage data unreliable for cross-country comparison.

### Cambodia (KHM)
