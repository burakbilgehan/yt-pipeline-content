## 3. Track 2: Non-OECD "Ugly Truth" Countries

### 3.1 Methodology Differences — ⚠️ CRITICAL DISCLAIMER

The OECD dataset uses a specific methodology:
- **Wages:** Full-time-equivalent average annual wages, PPP-adjusted using OECD's own PPP conversion factors
- **Hours:** Average annual hours actually worked per worker, from national accounts

For non-OECD countries, **no equivalent dataset exists.** We must use proxies:

| Metric | OECD (our main chart) | Non-OECD proxy | Comparability |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|--------------|
| Wage/Income | OECD Average Annual Wages (PPP) | GNI per capita PPP (World Bank) or ILO wage data + PPP conversion | ⚠️ LOW — GNI includes non-wage income; ILO coverage varies |
| Hours | OECD ANHRS from national accounts | Our World in Data / ILO / Penn World Table | ⚠️ MODERATE — methodology differs, informal sector treatment varies |

**Key differences that make direct comparison problematic:**
1. **GNI per capita ≠ Average wage** — GNI includes investment income, transfers, and is divided by total population (not just workers)
2. **Informal sector** — In countries like India (80% informal), Bangladesh, Nigeria, official wage stats capture only the formal sector. Including informal workers would push wages much lower and hours higher.
3. **Hours data** — ILO/PWT hours estimates for developing countries often rely on labor force surveys with limited coverage
4. **Age of data** — Some figures are 2022-2024, others estimated

**The video MUST include a disclaimer when showing these countries.** See Section 5.

## 4. Recommended 5-6 Countries for the Video

### Tier 1 — Must Include (strongest narrative + data)

1. **India** (IND) — ~$8,600, 2,383h, $3.61/hr
   - **Why:** World's most populous country. Everyone has a mental model of India. Huge contrast with OECD.
   - **Narrative:** "1.4 billion people. $3.61 per hour. And that's the formal sector average."

2. **Bangladesh** (BGD) — ~$7,000, 2,383h, $2.94/hr  
   - **Why:** Garment industry supplies Western fashion brands. The clothes you're wearing might have been made by someone earning $2.94/hr.
   - **Narrative:** Direct consumer connection.

3. **Cambodia** (KHM) — ~$5,500, 2,389h, $2.30/hr
   - **Why:** Highest hours AND lowest income in entire dataset. The absolute bottom-left extreme.
   - **Narrative:** "2,389 hours per year — 1,049 more than Germany — for $5,500."

4. **Pakistan** (PAK) — ~$7,000, 2,471h, $2.83/hr
   - **Why:** SECOND-HIGHEST hours in the world. Combined with low income = extreme TRAP.
   - **Narrative:** "Pakistan works 245 more hours than Mexico — and earns a third as much."

### Tier 2 — Strong Candidates (pick 1-2)

5. **Nigeria** (NGA) — ~$5,800, 2,062h, $2.81/hr
   - **Why:** Largest economy in Africa. Oil wealth doesn't reach workers. Good for geographic diversity.
   - **Narrative:** Africa's largest economy, $2.81/hr.

6. **Philippines** (PHL) — ~$11,000, 2,096h, $5.25/hr
   - **Why:** Large English-speaking audience connection. Overseas Filipino Workers angle — people leave because domestic wages are so low.
   - **Narrative:** Migration story.

### Tier 3 — Interesting but Less Impactful

7. **Indonesia** (IDN) — $12,000, 2,020h, $5.94/hr — Moderate impact, close to Philippines
8. **Vietnam** (VNM) — $13,000, 2,160h, $6.02/hr — Growing economy, less shocking
9. **Egypt** (EGY) — $14,000, 2,369h, $5.91/hr — High hours but income not as low
10. **South Africa** (ZAF) — $14,000, 2,181h, $6.42/hr — Inequality story more complex

### **RECOMMENDED FINAL 6:**
**India, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Pakistan, Nigeria, Philippines**

These 6 give:
- Geographic diversity: South Asia (3), Southeast Asia (1), Africa (1), multiple subregions
- Income range: $5,500 - $11,000 (all dramatically below Mexico's $20,433)
- Hours range: 2,062 - 2,471 (all at or above Mexico's 2,226)
- $/hr range: $2.30 - $5.25 (all below Mexico's $9.18 — most less than half)
- Strong narrative hooks for each

## 6. Killer Stats for the "Ugly Truth" Segment

1. **Cambodia vs Germany — the extreme of extremes:**
   - Cambodia: $5,500/yr, 2,389 hours. Germany: $69,433/yr, 1,340 hours.
   - Germany earns **12.6× more** while working **1,049 fewer hours**.
   - Germany earns **$51.82/hr** vs Cambodia's **$2.30/hr** — a **22.5× difference**.

2. **Pakistan vs Luxembourg — the poles of the planet:**
   - Pakistan works **2,471 hours** (the most in our dataset). Luxembourg works **1,473** (among the least in OECD).
   - Pakistan earns **$2.83/hr**. Luxembourg earns **$64.12/hr**. That's **22.7× more per hour, while working 998 fewer hours per year**.

3. **Mexico as the bridge:**
   - Mexico was OECD's worst at $9.18/hr. But Mexico earns **2-4× more per hour** than India, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Pakistan.
   - "The bottom of the OECD club is still the top 25% of the world."

4. **India — the scale:**
   - 1.4 billion people. $3.61/hr PPP. That's **less than 7% of Luxembourg's hourly rate**.
   - An Indian worker would need to work **17.8 years** at Indian hours to earn what a Luxembourg worker earns in ONE year.

5. **Bangladesh — the supply chain:**
   - The shirt you're wearing may have been made by someone earning $2.94/hr — working 2,383 hours per year.
   - A German worker earns the Bangladeshi worker's ENTIRE annual salary ($7,000) in about 5 weeks.

6. **The cluster visualization:**
   - All 6 ugly truth countries would appear in a tight cluster at the **far bottom-right** of the chart.
   - They'd be so far from even Mexico that you'd need to zoom out the chart — which is exactly the visual impact we want.
