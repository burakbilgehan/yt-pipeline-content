This video breaks down the 5 maritime chokepoints that control global trade: the Strait of Hormuz (currently blockaded), the Strait of Malacca (#1 by volume), the Suez Canal (60% below normal traffic), the Panama Canal (facing a drought crisis), and Bab el-Mandeb (zero LNG transit). For each chokepoint, we cover the geography, daily oil and gas volume, historical crises, price impact, and current 2026 risk level. We end with the cascade effect — how disrupting one chokepoint forces rerouting through the others, compounding delays and costs across global shipping routes.
