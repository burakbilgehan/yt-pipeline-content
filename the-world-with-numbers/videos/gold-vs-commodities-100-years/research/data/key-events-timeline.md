# Key Events Timeline: Gold vs Commodities
> version: 1
> date: 2026-03-15

## Timeline of Events Affecting Gold/Commodity Ratios

| Year | Event | Impact on Gold | Impact on Ratios |
|------|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1925 | Gold standard era | Gold fixed at $20.67/oz | All ratios stable, reflecting true supply/demand |
| 1929 | Great Depression crash | Gold fixed, stocks crashed | Dow/Gold crashed from 18.4 to 2.0 (1932) |
| 1933 | FDR bans private gold ownership (Executive Order 6102) | Gold confiscated from citizens | Market disruption |
| 1934 | Gold Reserve Act — gold revalued to $35/oz | +69% instant revaluation | All commodity/gold ratios dropped ~40% overnight |
| 1944 | Bretton Woods Agreement | Gold at $35/oz becomes global standard | Stability era begins |
| 1945 | End of WWII | Gold stable | Commodities begin post-war recovery |
| 1960s | Vietnam War spending, inflation building | Gold still fixed at $35 but pressure building | Ratios artificially high (gold underpriced) |
| 1971 | **Nixon Shock** — end of gold convertibility | Gold begins free-floating, rises sharply | THE pivotal moment — all ratios begin declining |
| 1973 | OPEC oil embargo | Gold surges, oil quadruples | Oil/Gold ratio temporarily rises |
| 1979-1980 | Iranian Revolution + Soviet invasion of Afghanistan | Gold peaks ~$850/oz | Dow/Gold hits historic low of 1.6:1 |
| 1980 | Hunt Brothers silver squeeze | Silver peaks $49.45/oz | Silver/Gold ratio compressed to 37:1 |
| 1980-2000 | **Great stock bull market** | Gold enters 20-year bear market | Dow/Gold ratio soars from 1.6 to 39.5 |
| 1999 | Dot-com peak approaching | Gold hits 20-year low (~$253/oz) | Dow/Gold peaks at 41.3 (Jan 14, 2000) |
| 2001 | 9/11 attacks | Gold begins new bull run | Ratios start declining |
| 2008 | **Global Financial Crisis** | Gold surges as safe haven | Oil/Gold spikes then crashes, Dow/Gold drops |
| 2011 | European debt crisis, US credit downgrade | Gold nears $1,900/oz | Silver hits $49.82 (April), Dow/Gold at 6.5 |
| 2020 | **COVID-19 pandemic** | Gold hits $2,075/oz (Aug) | Oil briefly goes NEGATIVE (-$37), Oil/Gold ratio collapses |
| 2022 | Russia-Ukraine war, inflation surge | Gold steady ~$1,800 | Commodities spike, some ratios temporarily rise |
| 2024-2025 | **Gold breaks all records** | Gold surpasses $4,000/oz (Oct 2025) | ALL commodity/gold ratios at or near historic lows |
| 2025 | Silver surge | Silver hits record $69.74 (Dec 2025) | Silver/Gold ratio improves to ~42:1 |
| 2026 Jan | Silver crosses $100/oz | Silver at $99-101/oz (COMEX) | Silver/Gold ratio at 42:1, still below 1925 level |

## Key Cycles

### Cycle 1: Gold Standard Era (1925-1971)
- Gold was FIXED — ratios reflect pure commodity supply/demand dynamics
- Commodities could freely move vs gold
- Most interesting: the 1934 revaluation instantly changed all ratios

### Cycle 2: Post-Nixon Shock (1971-1980)
- Gold freed, skyrocketed
- ALL commodity/gold ratios collapsed
- Dow/Gold went from 27.7 (1965) to 1.6 (1980)
- The "great gold decade"

### Cycle 3: Stock Bull / Gold Bear (1980-2000)
- Longest stock bull market in history
- Gold lost 70% from peak
- Dow/Gold ratio expanded from 1.6 to 39.5 (24x expansion!)
- Most commodities also gained vs gold during this period

### Cycle 4: Gold Resurgence (2000-2025)
- Gold went from $273 to $4,200 (+1,438%)
- Dow/Gold ratio back down from 39.5 to 10.0
- Oil/Gold ratio collapsed to historic lows
- Platinum fell BELOW gold for first time and kept falling
