# Video Critique v2 — Duration & Pacing Analysis
**Date:** 2026-03-17
**Reviewed:** script-v3.md (v3.2), TTS audio durations (7 acts), channel-config.json
**Scope:** Duration and pacing ONLY (not content quality, visuals, or audio)
**Overall Grade:** B-

## Executive Summary
At 9:20, this video is ~50 seconds over the 8:30 target but that alone isn't the problem. The real issue is **uneven act distribution**: Act 2 is a 2-minute monolith covering 19 years while Act 3 covers 27 years in just 64 seconds. This asymmetry creates a sluggish first third that will kill retention on a channel with zero subscribers and zero trust. For a seed channel's FIRST video, 8:00–8:15 is the ideal target. The narrative arc is strong — this is a trimming problem, not a rewrite problem.

## Strengths
1. **The hook is tight.** 60s for Act 1 — the "26,000% → 132%" reveal is efficient and gripping. No fat.
2. **Act 4 (Nixon Shock) is perfectly paced.** 65s for the single most dramatic event. Dense, punchy, visual-friendly. Don't touch it.
3. **Act 3 is lean and effective.** 64s to cover the post-war boom, introduce 5 new assets, and set up the crisis. Well-compressed.

## Issues Found

### 🔴 Critical (must fix before publish)

1. **Act 2 is 120.76s (2:01) — the longest act by 40+ seconds, and it's the SECOND act.**
   At this point in the video, you haven't earned viewer trust yet. A 2-minute act this early is a retention killer. The content covers: (a) gold standard explanation, (b) Roaring Twenties boom, (c) Great Depression crash, (d) FDR's gold revaluation + silver impact, (e) World War Two stagnation, (f) Bretton Woods. That's **four narrative beats** crammed into one act.

   **Diagnosis:** The FDR/silver paragraph and the WWII/Bretton Woods paragraph are the bloat. The silver detail — while interesting — is a tangent that breaks momentum right after the Depression crash (the emotional peak). The WWII paragraph says "markets freeze, ratio barely moves" then spends 2 more sentences on Bretton Woods context the viewer doesn't need narrated.

   **Fix — Cut ~30 seconds from Act 2 (target: ~90s):**

   - **CUT the silver detail entirely** (saves ~10-12s of speech): _"And here's a detail most people miss — silver gets crushed by this move. When gold's price nearly doubles but silver barely moves, the silver-to-gold ratio drops forty-six percent. Gold eats silver's lunch."_ — Non-essential. Silver's decline can be shown visually on the chart without narration. The viewer doesn't need a silver subplot in the second minute.

   - **TRIM the WWII/Bretton Woods paragraph** (saves ~12-15s): Replace the current 4-sentence paragraph:
     > _"Then comes World War Two. Markets freeze. The ratio barely moves — hovering around three for years. The whole world is busy fighting. But in 1944, forty-four nations gather at a hotel in New Hampshire and create a new global financial system — Bretton Woods. Every major currency is now pegged to the dollar, and the dollar is pegged to gold. Gold becomes the foundation of the entire world economy."_

     With:
     > _"Then comes World War Two. Markets freeze — the ratio flatlines at three for a decade. In 1944, forty-four nations create Bretton Woods — pegging the entire world economy to gold."_

   - **TRIM the Depression emotional filler** (saves ~5-8s): Cut _"Millions lose everything. Banks collapse. A generation that bought stocks at the top watches them lose almost ninety percent of their real value."_ — This is a data channel, not a history documentary. "Crashes from eighteen down to two" is devastating enough. The chart IS the emotion.

   **Net effect:** Act 2 goes from ~121s → ~85-90s. Total video drops to ~8:45-8:50.

### 🟡 Important (strongly recommended)

2. **Act 7 is 81.97s (1:22) for a scoreboard + closing — about 15-20s too long.**
   The scoreboard reveal is the payoff and should feel punchy. Currently it has five narrative blocks: (a) overview sentence, (b) Nikkei winner story (4 sentences!), (c) rest of scoreboard, (d) cyclical observation, (e) philosophical closer + CTA. The Nikkei paragraph alone is practically a mini-act.

   **Fix — Cut ~15-20s from Act 7 (target: ~62-65s):**

   - **TRIM Nikkei paragraph** (saves ~8-10s): Replace:
     > _"It started from almost nothing, went eighty-four times higher, crashed eighty-eight percent, and still ended up the biggest winner at more than ten times its starting value. The rise is absurd. The crash is absurd. And winning despite the crash — that might be the most absurd part."_

     With:
     > _"It started from nothing, went eighty-four times higher, crashed eighty-eight percent — and still ended up on top. Absurd."_

     Let the scoreboard visual carry the weight.

   - **CUT the cyclical observation paragraph** (saves ~10-12s):
     > _"The Dow-to-Gold ratio is cyclical — it swings between one and forty-five over decades. Right now, we're at about fourteen. Is it heading back to forty-five? Or back to one? History says both are possible."_

     This is a SECOND closing after the scoreboard. It deflates the payoff moment. The "YOU ARE HERE" visual can communicate this without narration — or move it to a pinned comment / Part 2 teaser.

   **Net effect:** Act 7 goes from ~82s → ~62-65s.

3. **Act 6 has a trimable FTSE tangent** (~8s savings).
   > _"Britain's FTSE — which peaked at nearly thirty-nine times gold in nineteen ninety-nine — falls all the way back to just under nine. Up only forty percent over its entire existence. Barely positive — after more than thirty years."_

   The FTSE detail is the weakest data point in Act 6. It breaks the escalation pattern (dot-com → 2008 → COVID → commodity destruction). Replace with: _"The FTSE? Up only forty percent — over its entire existence."_ — One sentence instead of three.

   **Net effect:** Act 6 goes from ~90s → ~82s.

## Why 8:00-8:15 Is the Right Target

- **Channel context:** Seed stage, zero subscribers, first video ever. You need to EARN the right to hold attention for 9+ minutes. An 8-minute banger builds more trust than a 9:20 video with 40% drop-off.
- **Content type:** Data visualization with horse-race chart. This format IS continuously engaging — but the narration doesn't need to describe what the viewer can already SEE. Every second of narration that describes the chart is wasted time. The best narration adds context the chart CAN'T show (FDR's decision, Nixon going on TV, Bretton Woods).
- **YouTube benchmarks:** Vox data videos: 6-10 min. Kurzgesagt: 8-12 min. For a new channel, landing at the lower end of the "premium data video" range is correct.
- **The mid-roll ad threshold is 8 minutes.** Staying above 8:00 preserves monetization eligibility. Cutting below 8:00 loses a revenue option.

## Final Verdict

**Do NOT publish at 9:20.** The pacing problem is concentrated in Act 2 — fix that and you're in acceptable territory.

**Minimum to ship:** Cut Act 2's silver tangent + WWII filler (~30s) → brings total under 8:50. Shippable.

**Ideal to ship:** All 🔴 + 🟡 fixes → 8:00-8:15. This is the sweet spot — above the mid-roll threshold, tight enough for a first video, and every remaining second earns its place.

**Do NOT cut below 8:00.** You need mid-roll eligibility, and the content is genuinely dense enough to justify the length.
