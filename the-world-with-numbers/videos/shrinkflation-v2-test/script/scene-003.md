Imagine you earned five hundred seventy-two dollars a week in two thousand. A dozen eggs cost ninety-one cents. Today you earn twelve hundred — but eggs cost four twenty-five. Did eggs get more expensive... or did your paycheck grow faster? <break time="500ms"/> That's the question behind what we call the Real Unit Price Index — RUPI. Take twenty-five years of Bureau of Labor Statistics prices — per pound, per gallon, per dozen — and divide each product's price growth by median wage growth.
