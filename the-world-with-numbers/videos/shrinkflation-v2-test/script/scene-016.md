Twenty twenty. <break time="500ms"/> A dip across the board. Every product appears to get cheaper overnight. But this isn't because food prices dropped. During COVID, lower-wage workers disproportionately lost their jobs — retail, hospitality, food service. The people who remained employed skewed higher-income. So the median wage jumped. Not because anyone got a raise. Because the composition of who was still working changed. <break time="500ms"/> The prices on the chart were real. The wage improvement was a statistical artifact.
