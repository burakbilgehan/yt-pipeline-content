## Key Production Notes

### Race Chart Architecture
The race line chart is a single Remotion component that evolves across scenes 006–019 and 021–025. It needs:
- 6 product lines with distinct visual treatment (max 2 vivid colors per brand guide)
- Eggs: Muted Pink #D8A7B1 (primary), Coffee: Muted Blue #90AFC5 (secondary)
- Other 4: Sage Silver, Accent Cream, dashed variants
- RUPI 1.0 baseline with red/green zones
- Year axis, RUPI axis (JetBrains Mono, tabular figures)
- Dot grid background
- Annotation overlay system for shrinkflation events, event markers
- Deflator indicator (top-right) for lens switch scenes
- Smooth line morph animation for deflator changes

### Critical Visual Moments
1. **Scene 006** — Race start glow/pulse (anticipation)
2. **Scene 013** — False happy ending vignette (tension)
3. **Scene 017** — All lines surge UP (urgency, faster animation)
4. **Scene 020** — "For whom?" pivot text (emotional hinge)
5. **Scene 023** — Lines JUMP under min wage (the gut punch)
6. **Scene 023** — 11 min vs 35 min clock comparison (tangible)

### Animation Standards (from brand guide)
- Default easing: bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)
- Charts: spring(stiffness: 100, damping: 20)
- Cards: spring(stiffness: 80, damping: 18)
- Line charts: strokeDashoffset reveal, 60–90 frames
- Number counters: interpolate 30–45 frames
- Stagger: 6-frame for chart bars, 10-frame for list items
- Transitions: 8-frame crossfade default
- Film grain: 3% opacity overlay on every scene

### Data Requirements
All RUPI values are sourced from research-v4.md. The race chart needs:
- Full 2000–2025 time series for 6 products under Median Wage deflator (scenes 007–019)
- Full 2025 snapshot under CPI deflator (scene 021)
- Full 2025 snapshot under Min Wage deflator (scenes 022–024)
- Full 2025 snapshot under Gold deflator (scene 025)

## Flags & Concerns

1. **Duration +4.1% over target** — within tolerance but noted. If TTS runs long, scene 015 (69.7s — longest scene) could be tightened.
2. **Scene 015 is very long** (69.7s, 143 words). It has 3 internal phases (skimpflation concept → chart return → ominous still). May need internal visual pacing to avoid monotony.
3. **6-product line chart visual complexity** — With 6 lines on screen simultaneously, visual clarity is a challenge. The brand guide limits to 2 vivid colors, so 4 products will need muted/dashed line treatments.
4. **Deflator morph animation** is the video's most technically complex element. The lines must visibly shift position while maintaining recognizable product identity. This needs careful Remotion implementation.
5. **No AI-generated images or stock media** — this is entirely Remotion-rendered (data viz + text overlays + animated components). This is appropriate for a data-driven video but means zero variety from external media. The visual variety comes from chart states, annotations, and the few non-chart scenes (003, 005, 015 phase 1, 020, 027).
